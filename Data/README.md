## Description of datasets

timelapse_doubling_time_inf_2025may5.csv: whole dataset of cell doubling time distributions including ancestor strains (petite and grande) and the 5 replicate populations of the anaerobic line for the 200, 400, 600 and 1000 days of evolution.

first_second_div_doubling_data_2024dec2.csv: same as timelapse_doubling_time_inf_2024dec2_clean.csv but only including the first and second division.

cell_sync_mother_daughter_2024dec2.csv: dataset for mother and daughter doubling times for comparison between them.
